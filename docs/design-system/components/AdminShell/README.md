The back-office frame. It replaces the single header that wraps **20 links** across the top, a wrapping user area and a footer reading "Sprint 7 (Publish)".

- **Sidebar (64rem and up).** 264px, white, hairline edge, sticky. Links are grouped by how the work flows: **Sell** (Orders, Discount codes, Delivery fees, Notify-me demand), **Fulfil** (Pick, Pack, Pick exceptions), **Warehouse**, **Make** (Vendors, Purchase orders, Production, Receiving and QC, Labelling), **Catalog**, **Storefront**, **Admin** (Staff, Roles, Audit, Notification log). A group is a disclosure button; only groups with at least one link the role may open are shown. The group holding the current page opens by itself and the open state is remembered. A collapsed group shows the total of its counts so nothing is hidden. Link: 44px, icon and label, count pill (marigold when it needs action), `aria-current="page"` with a 3px ink edge.
- **Top bar.** Breadcrumb (where am I), a "Go to" box that jumps to any page the role may open (`Ctrl K`), the alert bell with a count, and the user menu: **Security**, **Sign out**, **Sign out everywhere** (the last is separated and red; today three buttons sit in the header).
- **Phones and tablets.** The sidebar becomes a left **drawer** (native `<dialog>`, focus trapped, Esc and scrim close, returns focus to the menu button). The top bar keeps the brand, the bell and the user. Stations (pick, pack) hide the chrome entirely (the app already supports a chromeless mode).
- **Content.** `adm-main` is max 1440px, 12 / 24 / 32px padding. Page order everywhere: breadcrumb, title and one sentence, actions (one primary), content.

Rules: the footer outbox badge moves to the Today page and the bell menu; remove the "Sprint" text. Keep `noindex, nofollow`. Menu groups and labels are proposals; routes and permissions are unchanged. "Go to" searches page names only, not records (record search is a separate decision).
