How to measure

  1. Height. Stand your child against a wall without shoes and measure from the floor to the top of the head.
  2. Chest. Wrap the tape around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms.
  3. Waist and hip. Measure around the natural waist, then the fullest part of the hips.
